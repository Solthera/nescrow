## Variant: Wallet light

### Design stance
A wallet you would keep open on a laptop: white surfaces, hairline rows, one accent,
and the escrow's terms laid out as a small statement rather than cards of equal weight.

### Key choices
- Layout: two columns on desktop (money + progress left, parties + terms right), single
  column on mobile with the action dock pinned
- Typography: system sans for prose, monospace tabular figures for every amount
- Color: white on light grey, one blue accent; status is text, not a badge
- Time: horizontal meter for the live window, vertical step list for the sequence
- Interaction: the dock collapses to a single primary action on mobile

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: desktop demo, readability, "serious product" credibility
- Weak at: less distinctive than the dark variant; risks looking like a generic fintech

### Best for
Judges viewing a screen recording on a laptop, and any reviewer who wants to read terms.
